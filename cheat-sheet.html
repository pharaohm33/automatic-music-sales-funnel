Influencer outreach

Micro-pages, negotiated volume

$10–251 story, 5K–15K followers
$25–50~2×/wk for the month, 10K–30K
$50–100~4×/wk for the month, 20K–75K
Negotiation opener Instead of one post, what would you charge for about 4 story shares a week for the month, same clip/link, swapped when I've got something new? Less work for you than a fresh post each time.
Non-negotiable Every story uses a native link sticker straight to the playlist — not "link in bio" — with a tracked link per creator.
Every email

One footer, reused everywhere

A quiet "Shop merch →" line in the same spot of every email's footer — welcome series included. It never competes with that week's topic and never reads as a pitch; it's just always there to click if they're already in a buying mood.
Offline version A QR code on the physical merch itself — tag, label, packaging — linking to the signup and to buy more. Same loop, no screen required.
Setup order

Build once, in this order

  • Create Beehiiv publication + signup form with source tags
  • Write 5-day welcome automation (Day 0/1/3/7/11)
  • Write 6 rotating weekly templates + schedule broadcast
  • Build the playlist — videos + vlogs, autoplay order
  • CTA is line one of every video description
  • Vet + DM 6–10 micro-pages, negotiate ~4×/wk
  • Confirm link stickers + tracked links, launch promo
  • Every link actually clickable — description, popup, bio, footer — never typed-out text
After it's running Set once. Then just add a new email to the rotation when you have something to say, or start a separate short automation for one-off things like a show announcement.
30×

~30:1 is email marketing's commonly cited return — once the list exists and sends are automated. Beehiiv's automation tools sit on paid plans (~$50/mo); the free plan works but means clicking "send" by hand on every email, indefinitely, not just at the start. Paid plans also add list segmentation — grouping fans by city, by interest, by anything you define — so a show announcement can go only to fans in that city instead of your whole list.
